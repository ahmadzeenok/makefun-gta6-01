PALMERA BAY

Rico Vega owes the Dockside crew $4,000.

Steal cars, outrun the Palmera Bay Police, and work off the debt — six missions across a living coastal city, from Marina Pier to the Old Lighthouse.

Progress saves automatically after every mission. No network needed — everything runs offline.

How to Play

WASD / Arrows — walk or drive  •  Shift — sprint
Mouse — aim  •  Click — shoot  •  F — punch
E — steal / enter a car, exit a car
Space — handbrake while driving
M — start the next story mission  •  P — pause

Crimes raise your wanted level — the PBPD sends more cars the hotter you get. Stay out of sight and out of trouble and the heat cools off. Follow the gold marker to your objective.
